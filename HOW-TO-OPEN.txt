Open Slot AI — pitch deck (offline)

1. Unzip this entire folder (keep index.html and the assets folder together).
2. Open index.html in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
   (Do not open inside Outlook/Word preview — use a browser.)
3. Advance: arrow keys, Space, or click. On the demo slide, press Play (~30s).

No install required. Works offline after unzip.

Presenter view (optional): press P in the browser.
